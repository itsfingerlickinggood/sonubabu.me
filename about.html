About

AI safety and security research, evaluation engineering, and software building.

Profile

I am an AI red teamer and AI safety researcher. I build the systems behind red teaming: attack agents that probe AI models and tool-using agents for weaknesses, evaluation harnesses that check what the AI actually did, and the measurement layer that shows whether the results can be trusted.

I built an engine that breaks AI models. It searches for attacks automatically, learns which ideas are working, and reports how often attacks succeed, with confidence intervals and checks on the judges that grade them.

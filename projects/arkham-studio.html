Arkham Studio

A product-positioning and interface-design exploration centered on human–AI interaction.

Concept

Arkham Studio explored a possible design practice for calm, legible interfaces around AI systems. The notes focused on translating uncertain model behavior into understandable controls, feedback, and interaction states.

What exists

The available portfolio material supports a concept and visual direction. It does not establish a registered studio, client work, shipped products, revenue, or a team. This page preserves the idea without presenting those outcomes as facts.

Design questions

  • How should an interface communicate model uncertainty and system state?
  • Where should users approve consequential actions?
  • How can safety and usability reinforce one another?

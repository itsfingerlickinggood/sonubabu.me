Personal intelligence terminal · Early concept

GOTHAM

What if Bruce Wayne’s underground command centre were software you could build for yourself?

The idea

GOTHAM began with Bruce Wayne’s underground workspace in the Dark Knight films: a private place to follow events, investigate connections and assemble a picture from scattered information. I imagined a personal intelligence terminal—something between Palantir and a Bloomberg Terminal, tailored to one person’s questions.

One place to make sense of things

The vision brings news, financial markets, company records and private research into a compact workspace. Search across sources, follow an entity, connect related events, and move from a headline to the evidence behind it. AI would help ask questions and synthesize material; source documents would remain available beside the analysis.

Built around the person using it

The interesting part is not a wall of dashboards. It is a workspace that remembers what you are investigating, lets you organize your own material, and makes useful connections without burying their provenance.

This is an early product and interface concept. The original page records the ambition; it does not represent a deployed intelligence system or access to restricted information.

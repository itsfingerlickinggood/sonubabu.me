Connected everyday services · Early concept

OpenX

One place for the parts of digital life that are usually scattered across a dozen apps.

Five parts, one home

OpenX imagines messaging, social, payments, health and shopping in a shared interface. The aim is to make ordinary tasks feel connected, without making the product feel crowded.

Connected, not tangled

A conversation might lead to a payment or a purchase. A health record might sit alongside a consultation. The interface should make those handoffs understandable while keeping each service’s data and permissions distinct.

Privacy as a design requirement

The vision is simple and privacy-first: clear consent when connecting a service, visibility into what is shared, and an understandable way to disconnect it. AI assistance belongs where it saves effort, with the person still in control.

The original landing page is an early expression of that vision, not a launched super-app. Its integrations, AI features and security promises are product intentions, not verified capabilities.
